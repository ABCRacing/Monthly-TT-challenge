Before you register

Do I need a new account?

No. Your Steam login is enough. There is no separate ABC Racing password.

When will my lap appear?

Once automatic registration completes, any eligible Steam Time Trial personal best for the month's car and track is picked up by our normal collector.

What becomes public?

Your chosen driver name, Steam ID64 and eligible Time Trial results may be published on the ABC Racing leaderboard and monthly archives. Requests are kept private while pending.

Can I leave later?

Yes. Contact the ABC Racing organiser to request removal or discuss your data. A dedicated contact address will be displayed in the registration form when sign-in opens.
